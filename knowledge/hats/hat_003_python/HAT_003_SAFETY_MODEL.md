## Safety Principles

- Detection examples are inert and educational.
- Human approval is required outside this pack for network, shell, package, credential, and file-system actions.
- Security-sensitive examples must stay non-operational and must not include exploit instructions.
- Real credentials must never be scanned, copied, printed, committed, or archived.
